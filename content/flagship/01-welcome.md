Welcome. This lesson is a placeholder that ships with the template — replace it
with your own.

Lessons live in `content/<course>/` as Markdown files. The number at the front of
the filename sets the order, so `01-`, `02-`, `03-` and so on. Everything between
the `---` lines at the top tells the platform about the lesson.

## What you can put in a lesson

Anything Markdown can do: headings, **bold**, lists, links, images and code.
